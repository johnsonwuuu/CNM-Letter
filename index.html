My darling Chi,

It’s crazy to think where we are now, starting from July 4th or even from 2 years ago since I’ve met you. I’ve told you I love you more than words can even describe, but sometimes I feel like these three words don’t even come close to how I feel for you. There are so many little things about you that I just adore and things that you probably don’t even realize I notice. I wanted to write this letter for you because I don’t ever want you to question how special you are to me and how grateful I am to have you in my life.

One of my favorite things about you is how you will randomly start laughing and smiling for absolutely no reason sometimes when you look at me. I don’t think you realize how contagious your happiness can be. You will just light up a room when you smile and laugh. Sometimes I’ll just find myself staring at you for too long and I’ll smile because seeing you happy genuinely makes me happy. It’s some of these little moments that remind me why I fell for you in the first place. You never have to do anything extraordinary to make my day better. Sometimes just being yourself is more than enough.

If there’s one memory I wish I could relive over and over again, it would be the moment you saw those 100 roses. I still remember how your eyes lit up and how surprised you were that I remembered something you had mentioned before. I don't think I'll ever forget the look on your face. What made that moment so special to me wasn't the roses themselves, but seeing how happy something so simple could make you. I love remembering the little things you tell me because the things that matter to you matter to me, too. If I could go back to that moment just to see you smile like that again, I would in a heartbeat.

Baby, there’s so much more to you than just your beautiful smile. One of the things I admire most about you is how much you care for the people around you. I’ve noticed how you go out of your way to make sure the people you care for are taken care of, and how you try to make everyone feel included. You have such a thoughtful and selfless side to you, and I don’t know if you give yourself enough credit for it. I hope you know that I see those things. I see how much effort you put into the people you care about, and I admire you so much for that. You deserve to feel that same kind of love and consideration in return.

I think I started realizing just how special you were becoming to me when making you happy became something I genuinely looked forward to. I would catch myself thinking about little things I could do for you, places I could take you, or ways I could make your day a little better. Not because I felt like I had to, but because I wanted to. Your happiness started to mean so much to me, and somewhere along the way, I realized that you had become someone I couldn't imagine not having in my life.

You know what’s funny, though? As much as I love our dates, our little adventures, and doing special things for you, some of the moments I look forward to most are the most ordinary ones. I think about what it would be like to go grocery shopping with you, walking down the aisles together, arguing over what snacks to buy, and figuring out what we’re going to make for dinner. I think about coming home after a long day and knowing you’re there. Being able to hold you, ask you about your day, and just enjoy each other’s company without worrying about when we’ll have to say goodbye again. I know those things might sound boring to some people, but with you, they sound like something I would never get tired of.

I don’t need every day with you to be some extravagant adventure. I want the quiet days, the lazy mornings, the random errands, and all the little moments in between. I want to keep learning about you, even the smallest things. I want to know what makes you smile on a bad day, what makes you feel loved, and how I can continue becoming someone you can count on.

Most importantly, I want you to know that I love you for who you are. Not just for the happiness you bring into my life, but for the person you are even on the days when you don’t feel your best. You don’t always have to be smiling or have everything figured out. I want to be there for those moments, too.

Thank you for letting me get to know you, for all the memories we’ve already made, and for giving me so many things to look forward to. I hope that as time goes on, I can continue showing you through the little things just how much you mean to me. If there’s one thing I hope you remember from this letter, it’s that loving you isn’t just about the big moments. It’s about finding happiness in the smallest parts of life simply because I get to share them with you.

I love you so, so much, baobei.
